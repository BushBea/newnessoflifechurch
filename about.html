Our heart

A place to belong, grow, and serve

We are a local church family shaped by faith in Jesus and a love for our neighbours. Whether you are exploring faith or have been part of a church for years, you are welcome to take your next step with us.

We make room for worship, Scripture-rooted teaching, honest connection, and practical care for the Akasia community.

Meet our pastor

Nkosinathi Rhulani

Founder and Lead Pastor

Pastor Nkosinathi planted Newness of Life Church with a passion for raising people to a new life in Christ — practical, Scripture-rooted, and rooted in community. He leads our Sunday teaching and oversees the church's vision and leadership team.
